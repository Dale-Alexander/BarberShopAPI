If you ever need to fire an admin:

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
	SET QUOTED_IDENTIFIER ON;
	UPDATE Users
	SET Role = 'CUSTOMER', TokenVersion = TokenVersion + 1
	WHERE Email = 'person@example.com';

	- Role = 'CUSTOMER' — they can never log in or reset password again
	- TokenVersion + 1 — their current session dies on their next click

	⚠️ SET QUOTED_IDENTIFIER ON is not optional. The Users table has filtered indexes, and without it the UPDATE silently does nothing and reports success. You'd think they were fired when they weren't. Run a SELECT afterwards to confirm.
