PaymentIntent
Think of it like an order ticket at a restaurant. 
When you tell the waiter what you want, they write it on a ticket and 
give it a number. That ticket tracks everything about your order — what it is,
how much it costs, and what stage it's at (pending, paid, failed). 
A PaymentIntent is the same idea but for a payment. 
You tell Stripe "I want to charge €20 in EUR via card" and Stripe creates 
a record that tracks that payment from start to finish.
It lives on Stripe's servers, not yours.

ClientSecret
When Stripe creates that PaymentIntent, it gives you the clientSecret:

A clientSecret — a short-lived token you send to the frontend

The clientSecret is essentially a claim ticket. It proves to 
Stripe that your frontend is allowed to complete this specific
PaymentIntent and no other. Your frontend passes it to Stripe's JS library
like so:  await stripe.confirmCardPayment(clientSecret, {
    payment_method: { card: elements.getElement(CardElement) }
});
```

Stripe then knows exactly which PaymentIntent to charge against.
